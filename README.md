## Research

### 🔬 Rain Garden Soil Health Assessment *(Senior Research)*
Assessed the ecological impacts of rain gardens on soil health and vital ecosystem functions across campus land-use types. Collected GPS-located soil samples and ran lab analyses including bulk density, pH, loss on ignition, and NPK, with infiltration testing in the field. Analyzed results using ANOVA and GIS mapping, wrote a 20+ page paper with a literature review, and presented findings to students and faculty.

🔗 [Paper](Rain_Garden_Soil_Health_Paper.pdf) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Poster](Rain_Garden_Soil_Health_Poster.pdf)

---

### 🌱 Biophilic & Resilient Design Research
Co-designed and built a 200 sq ft on-campus rain garden capturing ~360 gallons of runoff per 1-inch rainfall event, planted with 11 native species. Collaborated with 9 peers and faculty, designed a Living Building Challenge-inspired learning center mockup in SketchUp, produced a 12-page public research website, and presented the project to stakeholders, students, and faculty.

🔗 [Research Website](https://biophilicandresilient.knoxabolitionlab.org/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Presentation](Biophilic_Resilient_Presentation.pdf) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Rendering](Learning_Center_Rendering.pdf)

## Academic Writing

### 📄 Wetland Loss in the Mississippi River Delta
Examines anthropogenic and climate drivers of wetland loss in coastal Louisiana, evaluating current sediment diversion restoration strategies. Proposes a function-centered adaptive management framework, arguing that restoration success should be measured by ecological metrics, not just land area gained.

🔗 [Presentation](Wetland_Loss_Presentation.pdf) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Paper](Wetland_Loss_Paper.pdf)

---

### 📄 Urban Flooding: Houston, TX vs. Lahore, Pakistan
Compares how urbanization, climate drivers, and governance strategies shape flood risk and mitigation in two rapidly growing cities. Analyzes how differences in impervious surface cover, drainage infrastructure, and planning approaches produce vastly different hydrologic outcomes.

🔗 [Paper](Hydrology_Paper.pdf)

### 📄 Soil Carbon Sequestration: Literature Review
In-depth review of microbial, physical, and policy factors influencing carbon storage in agricultural soils. Synthesizes recent research to evaluate soil health, microbial efficiency, and economic barriers to large-scale implementation.
